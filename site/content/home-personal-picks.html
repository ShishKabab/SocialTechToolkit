Awards

The explanation by Samo Burja of awards made social technologies click for me. An award seems so ordinary that we barely think about it. But by shaping what people admire and aspire to, it can direct the efforts of whole communities—even societies. Something familiar suddenly became a way to coordinate large groups of people.

Mentorship

Christine Peterson’s advice made opportunities to grow and pursue big ideas feel within my reach. Asking for help, bringing people together, and finding ways to contribute could open relationships I hadn’t thought possible. Mentorship became something I could help make happen.
