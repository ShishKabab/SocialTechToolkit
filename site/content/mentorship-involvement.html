Asking for advice can give someone a way to become interested in your work. A request for funding asks them to make a decision; a question about a difficult problem invites them to think alongside you. They can get to know the project, see how you respond to feedback, and discover a contribution they would like to make.

A conversation that begins with advice may lead to an introduction, an offer to collaborate, or financial support. That is the insight behind the saying “ask for advice, get money.” The useful part is the opportunity to develop understanding and trust. Ask about something you genuinely want help with, and let further involvement be their choice.
