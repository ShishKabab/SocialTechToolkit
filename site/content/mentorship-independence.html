The aim is for the mentee to become more capable of acting and judging for themselves. That requires room to disagree. A mentor can offer advice generously without treating obedience as the price of continued support.

Nor does one person have to provide everything. The person who helps you improve your craft may be different from the person who helps you lead a team or find your next opportunity. What matters is whether these relationships help you understand more, attempt more, and eventually take responsibility without needing someone else to make every decision.
